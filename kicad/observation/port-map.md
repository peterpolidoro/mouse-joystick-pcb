## Channel notes

- DIG1: Boot beacon data or runtime QS TX depending on phase/build; no independent runtime trigger while tracing.
- DIG2: Tap is on the MCU side of the pyControl boundary series resistor.
- DIG3: External host can override the passive pull-up. This is board RX despite the DIO-A connector name.
- DIG15: Conditioned limit switch goes to the TMC, not an MCU GPIO.
- DIG21: A low passive bias is not proof of a brake fault while the switched domain is off.
- DIG22: Recipe/profile alias DAQ_SDA maps to functional DAC_SDA through the board source.
- DIG23: Recipe/profile alias DAQ_SCL maps to functional DAC_SCL through the board source.
- DIG24: MCU-side command tap; does not directly measure the switched HV rail.
- DIG25: No external DC pull reaches this tap; internal DIAG_EN behavior is not inferred by the resistor model.
- DIG26: Biased toward +3.3V_MCU through protection-series and pull-up resistors; not a guaranteed fault verdict during supply transitions.
- DIG27: R607 7.87k pulls the switch enable high; outputs from firmware or a debugger can override it.
- DIG28: Conditioned encoder A goes to the TMC, not an MCU GPIO.
- DIG29: Conditioned encoder B goes to the TMC, not an MCU GPIO.
- DIG30: Direct buffer input, no input tap resistor. Pull-up source is +5V_SW.
- DIG31: Direct buffer input, no input tap resistor. R1902/R1903 form the 12 V divider; DIG31 is connected, not grounded.

## Limits

- Connectivity is checked against exported XML and saved PCB pad assignments, not copper geometry.
- Resistor values and population are current schematic declarations, not a manufacturing/assembly comparison.
- Bias scenarios assume all attached drivers are high impedance and the declared rails have settled.
- Resistor paths describe topology; mixed rails require a divider/leakage/threshold analysis.
- Observed logic, reset transitions, leakage, probe loading and power-off behavior are not qualified.
- An unpowered observation buffer does not provide a valid logic reading, even for grounded inputs.
- Direction/domain labels are board declarations; supply checks and pin endpoints are connectivity evidence.
